We are thrilled to welcome **Sher Glaser, MSN, APRN, PMHNP-BC** to the Twilight Psychology team as our newest Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner. Sher is now accepting new clients for medication management, with **immediate openings for Medicare and self-pay clients** in Kentucky.

## About Sher

Sher is a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner with more than 25 years of nursing experience. Before transitioning into psychiatric practice, she built a distinguished clinical career in intensive care, interventional radiology, and neurology — settings that gave her a deep and nuanced understanding of how physical health, medication effects, and laboratory findings intersect with mental wellness.

She completed her Master of Science in Nursing at Northern Kentucky University in December 2025 and earned her PMHNP-BC board certification in January 2026. During her clinical training at Norton Behavioral Health, Lee's Specialty Clinic, and Baptist Neurology Associates, she gained hands-on experience with DSM-5-based diagnosis, psychopharmacology, and medication management across a range of conditions — including ADHD, autism, anxiety, depression, bipolar disorder, and personality disorders.

Sher brings a calm, patient-centered approach guided by shared decision-making and careful monitoring of treatment response and side effects. She has a particular interest in **GeneSight pharmacogenomic testing** to help tailor medication choices to each individual's unique biology.

## What Sher Offers

Sher provides psychiatric medication evaluation and ongoing management via **telehealth across Kentucky**. She is a strong fit for clients who:

- Are seeking medication management for ADHD, anxiety, depression, or mood disorders
- Have neurodevelopmental conditions such as autism and want a provider experienced with that population
- Are covered by **Medicare** and looking for an in-network PMHNP
- Prefer **self-pay** and need an appointment without a lengthy wait

## Insurance and Fees

Sher is currently **in-network with Medicare**. Self-pay clients are also welcome, with fees consistent with our standard medication management rates. Contact our office for current self-pay pricing.
